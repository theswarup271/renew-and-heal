Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between this and traditional therapy?

Traditional psychotherapy often diagnoses clinical mental illnesses and delves deeply into pathology. Our consultancy focuses on compassionate human presence, emotional de-escalation, life reorganization, and day-to-day companioning. If we feel you require psychiatric treatment, we will warmly assist you in finding qualified clinical professionals.

What if I start crying or don't know what to say?

Silence is welcome here. Crying is welcome here. You are never obligated to perform or speak eloquently. Our consultants are trained to hold comfortable, calm silence until you feel ready.

Can I stay completely anonymous?

Yes. You are welcome to use a preferred nickname or alias, disable your camera, and connect through encrypted voice or text services. Your peace of mind comes first.
